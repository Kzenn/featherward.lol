Privacy policy

Updated on October 4, 2026.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up. Featherward doesn't ask you for anything.
  • Your stats stay on your computer. The app doesn't send them anywhere.
  • No trackers. Neither in the app nor on this site: no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
  • Shared statistics are anonymous. They come from games where no player identifier is kept.

Who is responsible

Featherward is published by Clément FRANCOIS, as a private individual, data controller for the processing described here. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights: contact@featherward.lol.

What the app reads on your computer

Featherward reads the local API of the League of Legends client, on your computer only:

  • the game phase (in champion select, in game or not) and the current champion select: picked and hovered champions, bans, assigned roles, summoner spells. During champion select, other players' identities are never read;
  • once the game has started, at loading: the champion and position of the ten players (and their Riot ID if the client provided it at that time, which it doesn't today);
  • your champion mastery, your last 20 games and your rank, for your profile and suggestions;
  • when you open a finished game: its details, players' Riot IDs included, and its timeline (gold, level and position of the ten players minute by minute, kills and where they happened, objectives). They're used for your game's analysis, timeline and heatmap, and stay in memory for the session, without being saved;
  • your client's language, to show Featherward in the same language;
  • your rune pages, only to find the "Featherward" page when you ask for an import.

This information is used for the calculations shown, done on your computer. It doesn't leave your computer. Featherward never reads the game's memory, and reads nothing during a game after it has loaded.

What the app stores on your computer

In the %LOCALAPPDATA%\featherward folder:

  • public Riot data in cache: the champion, item and rune catalog, icons and artwork;
  • the shared statistics received for your server and rank;
  • your display preferences (window size, selected tabs, settings) and your language (file langue.json);
  • the players of your last 200 games read at loading: for each one, their team, champion and position (and their Riot ID if it was provided), without any account identifier (file identites.json), for your match history. They never leave your computer.

The "Clear the image cache" button in the settings deletes part of it; deleting this folder deletes everything.

What the app writes to your client

Only when you click "Import", during champion select: a rune page named "Featherward" (created or updated, never one of yours), or your summoner spells. Nothing else, never automatically.

Shared statistics

The builds, runes and matchups shown are computed from recent ranked games, fetched through Riot Games' official API. For each participant of a game, only the following are kept:

  • champion, role, team and result;
  • runes, summoner spells, skill order, and items bought with the minute they were bought;
  • for the game: its technical identifier, patch, duration, date and the rank tier where it was found.

Never kept: a player's name or Riot ID, nor their account identifier. To avoid going through the same player twice, the service only keeps a fingerprint of that identifier (HMAC-SHA-256), computed with a secret key kept separately, and with no link to the recorded games. Without that key, nobody can check whether a given player is in there; it is pseudonymization, not strict anonymization.

This raw data is kept while the statistics of its patch and the next one are computed, then deleted. Only aggregated statistics, which no longer concern any player, are published.

This processing relies on legitimate interest: producing general game statistics without identifying anyone.

This site

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party service: even its font is hosted here. Its host, GitHub, keeps technical logs like any host (including visitors' IP addresses) to run the service and keep it secure.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and restriction provided by the GDPR. Since Featherward keeps no data that identifies you, it generally can't find anything about you; you can still write to the contact address above. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
