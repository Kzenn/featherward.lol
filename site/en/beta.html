The Featherward beta

Before 1.0, a few dozen players try Featherward on their real games, League and TFT. Free, no account, and your stats stay on your PC.

Join the beta

Email contact@featherward.lol with your server (EUW, NA…) and what you play (Solo/Duo, Flex, ARAM, TFT). You'll get the download link back. Featherward installs itself, with no administrator rights, and updates itself.

What we'd like from you

  • Play as usual: the window opens during champion select, and closes afterwards.
  • Try the rune and summoner import, the profile, match history and game analysis, and for TFT the profile, Info tab, comp builder and meta.
  • Tell us what's missing, what's in the way, what's wrong.

Report a problem

In Featherward: Settings, Report a problem. The report holds the version, memory use and the end of the local log, with no Riot ID or account identifier. You read it, then "Copy and write an email" opens your mail app: paste the report and describe what happened. Featherward never sends anything itself.

What Featherward doesn't do

It never reads the game's memory, reads nothing during your games once loaded, and doesn't read other players' identity during champion select. Everything it reads, keeps and downloads.
