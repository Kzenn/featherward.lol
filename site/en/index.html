What it does

Featherward works before and after your game. During the game, it does nothing.

In champ select, before you lock in

Until you lock in, you see your champions ranked for this game: their win rate against your likely opponent, their win rate at your rank, and what they bring when your team is short on magic damage or frontline.

Up top, both teams with their roles and the enemy ultimate cooldowns.

Once you've locked in

Your champion takes center stage with the most played build at your rank, and the one that wins the most when they differ. Every number comes with its game count.

Runes and summoners import in one click, into a separate "Featherward" page. Your own pages stay untouched.

After the game

Open a game from your match history. Featherward picks out what went well and up to three things to work on, each with the exact moment it happened: a death far from your team, a drake lost right after, gold sitting in your pocket, a missed teamfight.

It doesn't grade you. It points at specific moments of your game.

The timeline

Objectives pinned on your team's gold curve, with the game's own icons: drakes, Baron, Herald, Voidgrubs, towers. Hover one to see who took it and who was there.

The heatmap

Where the kills happened, where you died, where you spent your time. Click a dot to replay the fight: who was there, and what it cost.

Your profile

Click the feather next to the clock: your rank, your recent form, your roles, your champions and your last games. It works outside champ select too.

How it works

Nothing to set up: Featherward reads your League of Legends client.

  1. Start it

    A feather shows up next to the clock. At rest, it uses about 5 MB of memory and no CPU.

  2. Queue up

    As soon as champ select starts, the window opens and follows your team's picks, bans and hovers.

  3. Play, then come back

    During the game, Featherward does nothing. Afterwards, open the game from your match history.

FAQ

Will it slow down my game?

No. At rest, it uses about 5 MB and no CPU. Its window only exists during champ select, and closes before the game, or when you open it yourself.

Where do the builds and matchups come from?

From recent ranked games at your rank and on your server, fetched with Riot's official API. The game count is always shown, and a build played too few times is never pushed forward.

Where does the game breakdown come from?

From your client, which keeps the details of your last 20 games. Featherward reads them when you open one and does the math on your PC.

Is it allowed by Riot?

Featherward follows Riot's rules for third-party tools: it only shows what the client already shows or general stats, unmasks no one, makes no decision for you and does nothing during the game.

Is it free?

The core of it, yes, and it will stay that way. Premium, at €3 a month or €30 a year, will add game recording and the Data Studio. See what's free and what's Premium.

What does it run on?

Windows, like the League of Legends client. In English or French, following your client's language.
