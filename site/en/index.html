What Featherward does

Before the game, champion select; after it, your profile and the analysis of your games. Nothing during the game.

Champion select at a glance

As soon as your champion select starts, Featherward comes to the front.

  • Your team and your opponents, their roles and damage profile.
  • Enemy ability and summoner spell cooldowns.
  • Before you lock in, "What to play?": your champions ranked against your likely matchup and at your rank, and what they bring to your team.
  • Once locked in, "Get ready": your champion front and center, its build, runes to import in one click, your matchup and the enemy ultimates.

What's played, and what wins

For your champion, at your rank: items, runes, skill order. The most played option, and the one that really wins, always with the number of games.

One click imports a rune page or your summoners. The page goes into a separate "Featherward" page: yours stay untouched.

After the game: what went well, what to work on

Open one of your games: Featherward finds your strengths and up to three areas to improve, each backed by a timestamped fact. Deaths away from your team, objectives lost after a death, unspent gold, missed teamfights, lane won or lost against your matchup.

No score, no comparison with others: just your game, and concrete advice.

The game timeline

Every objective pinned on your team's gold lead, with the game's own icons: drakes, Baron, Herald, Voidgrubs, towers. Hover to see who took it and who helped.

The heatmap

Where the kills happened, where you died, where you were. Click a point to replay the fight: who, with whom, and what it cost.

Your profile, whenever you want

Click the feather next to the clock to open your profile, even outside champion select: your rank, your form, your roles, your champions and your recent games.

How it works

Nothing to set up: everything comes from your League of Legends client.

  1. Launch Featherward

    A feather appears next to the clock. Featherward waits without using anything: about 5 MB of memory, and no activity while nothing happens.

  2. Enter champion select

    The window opens by itself and follows champion select live: picks, bans, your team's intents.

  3. Play, then come back

    During your game, Featherward does nothing. Afterwards, open it in your match history for its analysis, timeline and heatmap.

Fair play

Featherward follows Riot Games' policies for third-party tools.

Nothing during the game

No reading of the game's memory, no in-game information. Featherward reads the players once, at loading, then nothing until the end.

Hidden players stay hidden

During champion select, Featherward never reads other players' identities. An anonymous player always stays anonymous.

No MMR

No MMR estimate, no hidden rating: only game statistics.

You decide

Several options offered, never a single one imposed. Featherward only changes your client when you click, and never your own rune pages.

Your data

Featherward was designed to know as little as possible about you.

No account

No sign-up, no login: Featherward reads your client, and that's it.

Everything stays with you

Your stats and game analysis are computed on your computer. Featherward doesn't send them anywhere.

Other players stay anonymous

Shared statistics come from games where no player identifier is kept.

No trackers

Neither in the app nor on this site: no cookies, no analytics, no ads.

FAQ

Does Featherward slow down my game?

No. At rest it uses about 5 MB of memory and no CPU. Its window only exists during champion select, or when you open it yourself; when opened by champion select, it closes before your game.

Where do builds and matchups come from?

From recent ranked games, at your rank and on your server, fetched through Riot's official API and aggregated. The number of games is always shown, and an option played too few times is never presented as "the one that wins".

Where does my game analysis come from?

From your League of Legends client, which keeps the details and timeline of your last 20 games. Featherward reads them when you open a game, and does all the work on your computer.

Does it comply with Riot's rules?

Featherward is designed to follow Riot's policies for third-party tools: it only shows what the client already shows or general statistics, never reveals hidden players, never decides for you, and does nothing during your game.

Is it free?

Yes, entirely.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows, where the League of Legends client runs. In English and French, following your client's language.
